# Goal

Research comfortable family daytime activities in a mid-sized European city
across three fixed topics — outdoor and parks, culture and museums, markets
and neighbourhood walks — and produce a comparison report that I will rank
myself.

Rules:

- One researcher per topic, 6–10 candidates each, with venue, area, why it
  suits a family with young children, practical notes, and sources.
- One synthesizer merges the topic lists into a single deduplicated table
  plus short provisional thoughts. The synthesis is explicitly non-binding:
  no overall winner, no numbered league table, no "you should". Language
  like "worth comparing" and "tension between" is what I want. I rank.
- The synthesizer's only job is the merged draft. It must produce the draft
  as soon as the three topic lists are in; any checking happens after it,
  by someone else, and is not the synthesizer's concern.
- After synthesis, audit the draft on two dimensions only: (a) every entry
  keeps its source and area fields, (b) each topic contributes at least 6
  entries or the gap is named. The coordinator accepts or requests one
  bounded revision based on those audits.
- Final output: the merged non-binding report plus the audit results. If an
  audit lane fails to produce a usable verdict, say so explicitly and stop
  safely rather than pretending the draft was checked.

Read-only research; no repository changes.
